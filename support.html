Frequently Asked Questions

How do I start a project with Napon Tech?
Tell us what you want to build through the Get Started page. We'll review your requirements and discuss the next steps with you.
How long does a project usually take?
The timeline depends on the type and complexity of the project. After understanding your requirements, we'll provide an estimated timeline.
What services does Napon Tech offer?
We provide services including web development, mobile app development, custom software, UI/UX design, cybersecurity, graphic design, and other digital solutions.
How much does a project cost?
Project pricing depends on the scope, features, complexity, and requirements. Contact us with your idea and we'll discuss the appropriate approach and pricing.
Do you provide support after a project is completed?
Yes. We can provide ongoing maintenance, updates, troubleshooting, security support, and other post-launch services depending on your needs.
What technologies do you use?
We choose technologies based on the requirements of each project. The technology stack may vary depending on the product, platform, performance, security, and scalability requirements.
