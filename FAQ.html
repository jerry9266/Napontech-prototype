Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about our services, processes, billing, and technical support.

General Information

What services does NaponTech offer?
We provide web development, mobile app development, custom software, UI/UX design, cybersecurity, graphic design, and other digital solutions.
Do you work with international clients?
We are based in Nigeria and work with clients locally and internationally.
Do you provide ongoing support after a project is finished?
Yes. We can provide ongoing maintenance, updates, troubleshooting, security support, and other post-launch services depending on your needs.

Projects & Development

How long does a project usually take?
The timeline depends on the type and complexity of the project. After understanding your requirements, we'll provide an estimated timeline.
What technologies do you use?
We choose technologies based on the requirements of each project. The technology stack may vary depending on the product, platform, performance, security, and scalability requirements.
Can you redesign an existing website or app?
Yes. We can review your current digital product and design a new interface or rewrite the code to improve its functionality.
Who owns the source code once the project is complete?
Once a project is completed and paid for, you own the source code and intellectual property for your product.

Pricing & Billing

How much does a project cost?
Project pricing depends on the scope, features, complexity, and requirements. Contact us with your idea and we'll discuss the appropriate approach and pricing.
How does the billing process work?
We usually divide payments into milestones based on project phases. We will agree on a payment schedule before development begins.
What payment methods do you accept?
We accept bank transfers and card payments depending on your location.
